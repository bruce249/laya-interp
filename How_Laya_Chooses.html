How Laya Chooses

2026-10-09 · Vignesh

Laya's options do compete, but order flips come from positional encoding, not competition; confidence is set by three late MLPs; and noul answers follow semantic label words because the [MASK] reads its label in local layers before passage evidence arrives.

At a glance

C3 and C4 held outright, C1 and C2 held in a narrower form than stated, C5 held partly; the one clean null is a confidence direction in the residual stream. All headline numbers replicate in fp32 and on held-out data.

C1 · Option competition vs order

Competition is real but does not cause order flips. Flips come from where an option sits (RoPE positions); competition flows option-text → option-text, then into the [MASK].

Setup: 2,000 multiple-choice items (ARC, CommonsenseQA, QASC, 3–5 options), cast as laya choice questions. Competition M = change in option i's centred logit when option j's tokens are replaced by length-matched filler.

  • Not a slot bias. Slot index explains 0.2% of order-induced variance; neighbour strength correlates at r ≈ 0.
  • Route. Patching shows option j's content leaving its own span by layer 12, peaking in option i's text tokens at layer 16 (recovery 0.53), and reaching [MASK]i at layers 17–22 (0.28 → 0.84). The two head layers add nothing.
  • No small circuit. Blocking cross-option attention in the top 5, 10 or 20 heads moves M from 0.371 only to 0.36, no more than random heads. The top competition heads (L0 h5–7, L4 h6–7) are shared with smolaya, likely from the ModernBERT init.
  • smolaya shows the same split at half strength: M 0.194, flips 17.7% → 1.0% with options blocked and positions shared. Shared positions were not tested without the attention block; an order-blind-RoPE-only run cut flips to 8% but collapsed accuracy to chance, so it is not used as evidence.

C2 · Choice vs confidence

Three late MLPs act as a confidence amplifier: removing them halves the logit scale and fixes most of the miscalibration without changing answers. No compact set controls the choice itself.

Choice = direction of the centred logit vector; confidence = its length. Components were mean-ablated one at a time, then chosen greedily.

  • Information vs scale. AUROC is unchanged, so the amplifier adds sharpness, not knowledge of which answer is right.
  • Timing. The choice direction stabilises by layer 22; in the direct lens the norm is only half its final size at layer 25 and jumps in the head layers.
  • Overconfidence is input-dependent. The best temperature rises from 1.8 to 3.0 across logit-norm quintiles and is 4.8 on ARC-Challenge vs 2.0 on ARC-Easy, so one global temperature cannot fix it.
  • Nulls. A residual direction that predicts confidence (r ≈ 0.7) changes it no more than a random direction when steered. The 3-MLP set does not shrink jailbreak confidence (ratio 1.03); only the 10-component set did (0.36).

C3 · Label-following

When the label word disagrees with the option it sits on, laya follows the word, and only for meaningful words. The [MASK] reads its label in local layers 10–14; passage evidence arrives at layers 21–23. Cutting one attention edge removes the failure.

Test: noul questions where each option's label word is swapped ("true: no, the statement does not hold"). A model reading the passage is unaffected; a model reading the label word picks the option the passage contradicts.

  • Replicated on 300 held-out BoolQ items (42% for true/false) and on ~680 fresh items written by gpt-5.6-luna and verified by gpt-5.6-terra (96% yes/no, 60% true/false, 1–3% symbols).
  • Distance. Pushing the passage 256 tokens further from the [MASK]s raises true/false label-following from 39% to 77%; A/B stays flat.
  • Fix. Blocking [MASK] → own-label attention in local layers only drops label-following to 4% (held-out 3%, LLM items 4%) with normal accuracy unchanged or higher (0.785 → 0.811 on LLM items). The same block in global or head layers does nothing.

The fix as a drop-in patch

Installed on a stock laya.load agent (default SDPA attention, bf16), the label guard removes label-following through the official predict API. It blocks noul [MASK]s from their label words in local layers only.

C4 · Jailbreak, injection and Hindi

As in LLMs, a jailbreak is decodable from layer 0 but decided late. In laya the decision travels prompt → question tokens → [MASK], and it keys on jailbreak style more than on the override instruction itself.

  • Baselines. Jailbreak AUROC 0.94 on 900 held-out natural prompts; injection AUROC 0.87 (deepset).
  • Decodable vs decisive. A linear probe reads the label at 92% from layer 0 (shuffled-label control near chance). Patching jailbreak-run activations into the control run recovers the decision from prompt tokens only through layer 15; question tokens relay it at layers 14–20 (recovery up to 0.79); the [MASK] holds only about 0.4 until the first head layer completes it.
  • Steering depends on the layer. Adding the jailbreak direction at the layer-20 [MASK]s raises benign prompts flagged from 23% to 61%, with an unrelated urgency question at 3% and multiple-choice accuracy unchanged. Subtracting it at the layer-12 prompt tokens lowers flagged jailbreaks from 98.5% to 72%. At layer 8 neither direction had a useful effect.
  • Style over instruction. gpt-5.6-terra rewrote 145 flagged jailbreaks to remove only the override; laya still flags 82% of them (log-odds drop 7.7 but stay positive).
  • Shared signal. Jailbreak and injection directions have cosine 0.71.
  • Hindi. laya-multilingual flags 52% of Hindi-translated jailbreaks vs 8.5% of controls. The English-fit direction barely reads Hindi (AUROC 0.56) or steers it, but projecting it out drops Hindi detection from 52% to 8.5%: necessary, not sufficient.

C5 · Training objective and SAE features

The position-driven order effect and the late-MLP confidence amplifier appear in fresh models trained with either objective, so they come from the architecture and layout. Laya's label-following does not: neither control shows it, so it comes from laya's training data.

  • Objective. Over 3 seeds, RLCD has 16% less competition than CE (mean abs(M) 0.375 ± 0.036 vs 0.446 ± 0.041, Welch p = 0.09) and lower calibration error (0.103 vs 0.129, p = 0.19). Both are trends, not significant. Accuracy (0.695 vs 0.709), reversal flips (15.0% vs 14.4%) and label-following (4% vs 4%) do not differ.
  • Amplifier. Greedy search finds a late-MLP confidence set in all 6 control models; every set contains layer 22 and the first head layer's MLP (index 28). Across all 6, blocking option attention leaves 15–17% flips and adding shared positions brings them under 2%.
  • SAE. A TopK SAE (16,384 latents, k = 32) on 201k [MASK] vectors keeps 95% (layer 21) and 99% (layer 28) of answers when it replaces the real activations. The top-25 choice and confidence features overlap 11–15 of 25, and ablating the "choice" features also shrinks confidence. Choice and confidence are not separate features; they separate only at the component level.

Related work

The ideas exist for decoder LLMs; what is new here is the encoder decision-model setting, the C1 contrast, the C3 timing race and fix, and the controlled retrain.

Caveats and deviations from the plan

  • Off-task casting. Laya was not built for multiple-choice QA (45% accuracy); C1 and C2 describe how it handles options, not a tuned QA behaviour.
  • Off-distribution controls. Masked-parallel conditions lower accuracy (0.35–0.41), so they bound dependence rather than cleanly ablate it.
  • C3 data. The plan's LLM-generated items were added after BoolQ, once the OpenAI key worked; both agree.
  • Rewritten jailbreaks were not checked by hand; some may still contain jailbreak framing.
  • SAE feature labels are contaminated (the labelling prompt showed whether each example won) and are not used as evidence. Its variance-explained figure is inflated by outlier dimensions; the answer-preservation check is the one that counts.
  • Retrain uses 3 seeds per arm, which is enough for the architecture findings but leaves the objective effects at trend level; it uses public data, not laya's training set.
  • Not done: "natural counterfactual" segment patching (the rewrites change token length), and the plan's browser-only workflow (runs were launched from the laptop CLI).
